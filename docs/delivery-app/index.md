# Delivery App

The Delivery App is the driver's tool — receiving the day's runs, seeing each order's delivery details and site contact, navigating to the drop, and confirming delivery with a photo and signature.

This section covers three things: setting up and logging in, using the app day to day, and working through a delivery.

## Setting up the app

Drivers are invited from the Backend Portal. When creating a new user whose role is **driver**, tick the **Send Delivery App Invitation** checkbox. An email invitation with a download link is sent to them.

![Sending a Delivery App invitation from the Backend Portal](../assets/hj-4350902-1._sending_invite_to_delivery_app_-_Attachment_1.jpg)

The driver opens that email on their phone and taps the link to install:

- **Android** — downloads from Google Play.
- **iOS** — downloads from the App Store.

## Logging in

When you open the Delivery App, you land on the login screen.

![The Delivery App login screen](../assets/da-login.png){ width="300" }

- Enter your mobile number. A one-time code (OTP) is sent to you by SMS.
- Enter the 6-digit code to log in.

## Logging out

- Tap **Settings** in the bottom navigation bar. The Settings page shows the driver's profile — name, phone and email.
- Tap **Log Out** at the bottom, then confirm.

![The Settings page, with Log Out at the bottom](../assets/da-settings.png){ width="300" }
