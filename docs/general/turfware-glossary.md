# Turfware Glossary

Plain-language definitions of the terms used across Turfware — the web app, the [Delivery App](../delivery-app/using-the-delivery-app.md) and the [Harvesting App](../harvesting-app/using-the-harvesting-app.md). Terms are listed A–Z.

## A

**Archiving** — Hiding a customer, user or record from active lists while keeping its history. Archiving clears clutter without deleting anything; archived items can be restored later.

## C

**Carrier** — A freight provider that moves orders from farm to customer — your own fleet or a third party. Carrier accounts and settings control how each carrier's freight is charged and mapped to your accounts.

**Confirmed** — An order that has been confirmed and pushed into the harvest, delivery and laying workflow. See [Orders Confirmed](../web-app/order-management/orders-confirmed.md).

**Cut sheet** — The day's list of turf to be cut, order by order, that the harvest team works from in the [Harvesting App](../harvesting-app/using-the-harvesting-app.md). "Cut Sheet" is also a quick-view dashboard in the web app.

**Cutting style** — How turf is cut: **Rolls** or **Slabs**, with a Slabs / SQM figure. Set as a default per turf variety.

## D

**Deposit invoice / final invoice** — When an invoice is split, the **deposit** is billed up front (often when a quote is accepted) and the **final invoice** covers the balance on completion.

**Dispatch location** — Where an order's pallets are staged and sent out from. The driver's pick list groups orders by dispatch location.

**Docket** — A printed run or delivery sheet ("Print Docket") the team can carry.

**Driver** — The field user who works deliveries in the [Delivery App](../delivery-app/using-the-delivery-app.md).

## F

**Farm** — A property where your turf is grown and harvested. Each farm holds its location and its paddocks.

**Farm group** — A bundle of farms and carriers under one name, with a daily cutoff time. Grouping helps you organise capacity and logistics, and filter the Planner by group.

**Forecast (5 Day Forecast)** — A forward look at cutting orders for the next five days, shown as a tab on the Harvesting App dashboard.

**Forklift operator** — The [Harvesting App](../harvesting-app/using-the-harvesting-app.md) role that moves and dispatches cut pallets, taking an order **On Loading Bay** then **Ready To Dispatch**.

**Freight** — The charge for delivering an order. Freight rates and freight/account mapping control how it's priced and posted to your accounts.

## G

**Global Search** — The quick jump-to search that finds customers, orders and more from anywhere in the web app. See [Global Search](../web-app/global-search.md).

**Grass / turf variety** — A type of turf, such as Empire, Winter Green or Sir Walter.

## H

**Harvester** — The [Harvesting App](../harvesting-app/using-the-harvesting-app.md) role that cuts the turf and marks each order **Harvested**.

**Harvesting App** — The iPad app the harvest and forklift team use to work through the day's cutting. See [Using the Harvesting App](../harvesting-app/using-the-harvesting-app.md).

**Harvest tag** — A tag the Harvesting App automatically stamps on an item when it's marked Harvested, so each cut can be traced back.

## L

**Lead** — A prospective order or customer captured for follow-up (for example, via **Add Lead** in the Harvesting App).

**Lock** — Freezing an order so key details — customer, segment, pricing, freight, deliveries and more — can't be changed by accident. Click the lock again to unlock and edit.

**Lost** — An order that's been cancelled or abandoned; closed but kept for reporting. See [Lost](../web-app/order-management/lost.md).

## M

**Market channel** — Where a customer came from. Helps you see which marketing channels are working.

**Meters Harvested / Meters RTD** — Progress figures on the Harvesting App dashboard: how much turf has been cut (Harvested) and how much is ready to dispatch (RTD).

## N

**Notification** — An automated email or SMS sent to customers or staff at points in the workflow (for example, a delivery confirmation). Managed under Notifications.

## O

**On Hold** — An order paused and pulled out of the cut sheets and delivery runs — typically for rain or site delays — and saved to its own list to resume later. An **On Hold** badge shows on the order. See [On Hold](../web-app/order-management/on-hold.md).

**Order** — A job to supply turf that moves through the workflow from creation to harvest, delivery and invoicing.

**Order workflow** — The stages an order passes through: [Quote](../web-app/order-management/quotes.md) → Quote Accepted → (Shopify Pending) → Orders Pending → [Orders Confirmed](../web-app/order-management/orders-confirmed.md), with [On Hold](../web-app/order-management/on-hold.md) and [Lost](../web-app/order-management/lost.md) as side states.

## P

**Paddock** — A block within a farm where a particular turf variety is grown and recorded.

**Pallet (Wood / Steel)** — How turf is stacked for transport. **W** is a wooden pallet and **S** is a steel pallet; counts show as harvested against total.

**Pallet code** — A short code identifying an order's pallet, shown as "Pallet #".

**Pending** — An order that's been created but not yet confirmed; it sits outside the operational workflow until confirmed. See [Orders Pending](../web-app/order-management/orders-pending.md).

**Pick list** — The driver's list of what to collect for a run, grouped by dispatch location.

**Pickup / pick-up location** — A collection point where a customer or carrier picks turf up instead of having it delivered.

**Planner** — A quick-view dashboard for scheduling the day's work.

## Q

**Quote** — A priced proposal you send a customer before it becomes an order. Quotes live on their own list and don't enter the operational workflow until they're accepted and converted. See [Quotes](../web-app/order-management/quotes.md).

## R

**Ready To Dispatch (RTD)** — The final harvest status: the cut pallets are staged and ready to go out. The dashboard's **Meters RTD** tracks progress. Set by the forklift operator.

**Route / routing** — Plotting a delivery run's stops on a map, numbered in the order they should be delivered. See [Delivering an order](../delivery-app/delivering-an-order.md).

## S

**Sawfish** — The payments and accounting system Turfware links to. Linking a customer to their Sawfish client keeps one clean record — and one payment history — across Turfware and your accounts.

**Segment** — The customer's pricing group (for example Wholesale, Retail or Trade). A customer's segment dictates the pricing they receive.

**Shopify** — The online-store integration. Orders placed on your Shopify store arrive as **Shopify Pending**, waiting to be actioned.

**Site contact** — The on-site person for a delivery, shown in the order details with a tap-to-dial phone number. See [Delivering an order](../delivery-app/delivering-an-order.md).

**Slabs** — A cutting style where turf is cut as slabs (as opposed to rolls), with a Slabs / SQM figure.

**SQM** — Square metres — the unit turf is measured, cut and priced in.

**Supply rate** — The rate a supplier charges for an item, set per category in the supplier's settings.

## T

**Truck** — A delivery vehicle. Orders are assigned to trucks to build the day's runs.

**Turfware** — The turf-management platform this manual covers, spanning orders, customers, harvesting, delivery, logistics, reporting and payments.

## V

**Volume pricing** — Tiered pricing where the unit price changes with the quantity ordered.
