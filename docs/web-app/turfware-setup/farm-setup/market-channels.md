# Market Channels

Market Channels are the sources your customers come from — for example *Shopify, Facebook Marketplace, Radio, Word of Mouth, Web Enquiry* and *Walk-in*. You pick a market channel on an order (and on the customer), which lets you see which channels drive your sales.

## Where to find it

Left-hand navigation → System Settings → Market Channels. Click Create Market Channel to add one, or click a row to edit.

## Setting up a channel

- Name — the channel name.

That's all a channel needs. It then appears in the Market Channel dropdown on orders and customers.
