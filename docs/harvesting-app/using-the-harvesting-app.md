# Using the Harvesting App

## Logging in and choosing a role

After signing in, choose the role you're working as:

![Choosing a role — Harvester or Forklift](../assets/ha-role-select.png)

- **Harvester** — cutting the turf.
- **Forklift** — moving and dispatching the cut pallets.

Pick one and tap **Continue to Dashboard**. Both roles see the same screens below; the role you choose is recorded against the work you do.

## The dashboard

The **Daily Orders** dashboard shows the day's cutting at a glance, broken down by turf variety.

![The Daily Orders dashboard — the day's cut list by variety](../assets/ha-dashboard.png)

- Use the arrows next to the date to change day. The top right shows who's logged in and their role.
- The **left panel** is an overall snapshot of the day:
  - **Total Orders** — harvested orders against the total for the day.
  - **Total Area (SQM)** — harvested area against the total.
  - **W Pallets / S Pallets** — harvested Wooden and Steel pallets against their totals.
  - **Meters Harvested** and **Meters RTD** (Ready to Deliver) — progress bars for how much is cut and how much is ready to dispatch.
- The **right panel** breaks the same figures down per grass variety (for example Empire and Winter Green), so you can track each one. Tap a card to focus on just that variety.
- Toggle to the **5 Day Forecast** tab at the top to see cutting orders for the upcoming five days.

## Working through the cut sheet

- Tap the green **View Cut Sheet** button at the top right to see the full list of orders (this also clears any variety filter).
- Tap a specific order to view its details.

## Updating an order

Open an order and tap **Edit**. A box appears where you can update:

- **Farm** — tap **Farm** and select the farm the turf is cut from.
- **Pallets** — set the pallet type (Steel or Wood).
- **Square metres cut** — enter the SQM value.

!!! note
    The Harvesting App doesn't have a separate order-confirmation step — orders are updated and marked off as they're cut and dispatched, rather than confirmed.
