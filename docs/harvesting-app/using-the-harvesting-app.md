# Using the Harvesting App

## Logging in and choosing a role

After signing in, choose the role you're working as:

![Choosing a role — Harvester or Forklift](../assets/ha-role-select.png)

- **Harvester** — cutting the turf and marking it harvested.
- **Forklift** — moving the cut pallets and marking them ready to dispatch.

Pick one and tap **Continue to Dashboard**. Both roles work from the same cut sheet, but each can only set the statuses that belong to its part of the job (see below).

## The dashboard

The **Daily Orders** dashboard shows the day's cutting at a glance, broken down by turf variety.

![The Daily Orders dashboard — the day's cut list by variety](../assets/ha-dashboard.png)

- Use the arrows next to the date to change day. The top right shows who's logged in and their role.
- The **left panel** is an overall snapshot of the day:
  - **Total Orders** — harvested orders against the total for the day.
  - **Total Area (SQM)** — harvested area against the total.
  - **W Pallets / S Pallets** — harvested Wooden and Steel pallets against their totals.
  - **Meters Harvested** and **Meters RTD** (Ready to Deliver) — progress bars for how much is cut and how much is ready to dispatch.
- The **right panel** breaks the same figures down per grass variety (for example Empire and Winter Green), so you can track each one. Tap a card to focus on just that variety.
- Toggle to the **5 Day Forecast** tab at the top to see cutting orders for the upcoming five days.

## Seeing the harvest list

Tap the green **View Cut Sheet** button at the top right to open the day's cut sheet — every order to be cut, with its truck, customer, farm, variety and SQM. (This also clears any variety filter you set on the dashboard.)

Tap an order to expand it. The expanded row shows a **status bar** across the bottom and an **Edit** button.

## Completing an order

You complete an order by moving it along its status: **Pending → In-Progress → Harvested → On Loading Bay → Ready To Dispatch**. Expand the order and tap the status you want to set. Each role can only set the statuses for its part of the job — tapping one that isn't yours shows a *not authorized* message.

### As a harvester

Work the order up to **Harvested**:

- **Pending** → **In-Progress** as you start cutting.
- **Harvested** once the turf is cut. When you mark an item Harvested, the app automatically stamps it with a **harvest tag** for the day, so each cut can be traced back.

The dashboard's **Meters Harvested** bar moves up as orders are marked Harvested.

### As a forklift operator

Once the turf is cut, the forklift operator takes it through to dispatch:

- **On Loading Bay** when the pallets are moved to the bay.
- **Ready To Dispatch (RTD)** once they're staged and ready to go out. The order row highlights to show it's RTD.

The dashboard's **Meters RTD** bar moves up as orders are marked Ready To Dispatch.

## Editing an order

Expand an order and tap **Edit** to correct its details. A form appears where you can update:

- **Farm** — tap **Farm** and select the farm the turf is cut from.
- **Pallet Type** — Wood or Steel.
- **SQM** — the square metres cut.
- **Pallets** and **#** — the pallet count and pallet code.
- **Notes** — any cut-sheet notes for the order.

Harvesters also get a **Drop** field for recording the drop against the cut. Save your changes when you're done.
