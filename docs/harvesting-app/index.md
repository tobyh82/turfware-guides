# Harvesting App

The Harvesting App is the iPad app your harvest and forklift team use to work through the day's cutting — seeing what needs to be cut, by variety, and marking it off as it's harvested and dispatched.

This section covers setting up and logging in, and using the app day to day.

## Setting up the app

The harvest team is invited from the Backend Portal. When creating a new user whose role is **harvester** or **forklift operator**, tick the **Send Harvesting App Invitation** checkbox. An email invitation with a download link is sent to them.

![Sending a Harvesting App invitation from the Backend Portal](../assets/hj-4350885-1._sending_invite_to_cutting_app_-_Attachment_1.jpg)

The user opens that email on the iPad and taps the link to install:

- **Android** — downloads from Google Play.
- **iOS** — downloads from the App Store.
